## 3. Clear space and minimum size

- **Clear space (X)** = 6.00 units on a 100-unit canvas (= 6.0% of the mark width). Keep this area free of any other element.
- **Minimum size**: 16 px (screen) / 12 mm (print). Below this, use the simplified variant.
- **Safe zone diagram**: `safe-zone.svg`

## 4. Variants

| Variant | File | When to use |
|---------|------|-------------|
| Full color | `mark.svg` | Default, on light backgrounds |
| Single color | `mark-mono.svg` | Foil stamping, engraving, screen printing, fax, one-color print |
| Reversed | `mark-reverse.svg` | Dark or colored backgrounds |

## 5. Monochrome legibility diagnosis

- Distinct tones after monochrome conversion: **1**

## 6. Misuse (prohibited)

- Do not recolor the mark with colors outside the specified palette.
- Do not stretch, condense, rotate, or apply perspective distortion.
- Do not add gradients, drop shadows, glows, or outlines.
- Do not place the mark on a busy background without the reversed variant or a solid panel behind it.
- Do not reduce below the minimum size or inside the clear space.
- Do not reassemble the primitives into a new composition.
